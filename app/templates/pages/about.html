How It Works

CareerLens AI Architecture & Methodology

We built CareerLens to help job seekers understand how automated screening software and hiring teams evaluate their resumes against job postings.

How We Calculate Match Scores

Instead of simply counting words, CareerLens evaluates your background across four areas:

  • Skills Match (40%): Compares both exact tools and related technologies you already know against the job's required and preferred skills.
  • Experience Relevance (25%): Evaluates your years of experience and background against the role's seniority requirements.
  • Keyword Coverage (20%): Checks whether meaningful domain terms from the job posting appear naturally in your resume.
  • Education Alignment (15%): Compares your degree level and field of study with the employer's stated educational requirements.

Multi-Stage Product Roadmap

Stage 1: Production Web Foundation Completed

Clean Flask application structure, modular blueprints, drag-and-drop file validation, and responsive dashboard views.

Stages 2–6: Parsing, Matching, Gap Analysis & Roadmaps Completed

Document text parsing (PDF/DOCX), job description analysis, multi-metric compatibility scoring, ATS checks, and personalized career roadmaps.

Stages 7–9: Database, Accounts & PDF Reports Completed

SQLite persistence, private user authentication with encrypted passwords, evaluation history, and downloadable 14-section PDF reports.

Privacy & Candidate Security

CareerLens treats your career data with care and confidentiality:

  • Your resumes and reports are private to your account. No other users can see them.
  • We never sell your contact information, work experience, or resume contents to third parties or recruiters.
  • You can delete any evaluation from your history at any time with one click.
