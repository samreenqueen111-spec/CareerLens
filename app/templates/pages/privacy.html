Privacy & Data Security

We believe your resume belongs to you. Here is how CareerLens protects your files, account, and personal data.

1. Private to Your Account

Every resume analysis you run is linked directly to your account. Other candidates, recruiters, or public visitors can never see, search, or access your evaluation results.

2. Safe File Handling

Uploaded PDF and DOCX documents are processed securely and stored with private names in non-public directories. Your files are never exposed over public web links.

3. We Never Sell Your Data

We do not sell candidate contact information, work history, or resume contents to recruiters, data brokers, or advertising networks. Your documents are used solely to give you accurate feedback and recommendations.

4. Delete Anything at Any Time

You have full control over your history. When you delete an evaluation from your history page, the record, score breakdown, and saved snapshot are permanently removed from the database immediately.
